A moody dwarf seizes a workshop and demands materials. Deliver them and you get a legendary artifact and a master crafter; fail and the dwarf goes insane.

## Workshops and the artifacts they make

Which workshop a moody dwarf claims depends on their highest moodable skill. Here's what each workshop can produce as an artifact.

### Metalsmith's Forge (or Magma Forge)

Moodable skills: **Armorsmith · Metalsmith · Metal Crafter · Weaponsmith**

- **Armorsmith:** mail shirts, breastplates, leggings, greaves, gauntlets, boots, caps, helms, masks, shields
- **Metalsmith:** doors, beds, chairs, tables, statues, chests, armor stands, weapon racks, cabinets, anvils, coffins, floodgates, hatches, grates, cages, barrels, buckets, animal traps, pipe sections, splints, crutches
- **Metal Crafter:** figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, chains, flasks, goblets, instruments, toys
- **Weaponsmith:** any weapon, trap components

### Mason's Workshop

*The wiki calls this "Stoneworker's Workshop".*

Moodable skills: **Mason · Miner · Stone Carver · Stonecutter**

- **Mason / Miner:** doors, beds, chairs, tables, statues, querns, millstones, coffers, armor stands, weapon racks, cabinets, coffins, floodgates, hatches, grates
- **Stone Carver / Stonecutter:** figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, goblets, instruments, toys

### Carpenter's Workshop

Moodable skills: **Carpenter**

- doors, beds, chairs, tables, statues, chests, bins, armor stands, weapon racks, cabinets, coffins, floodgates, hatches, grates, cages, barrels, buckets, animal traps, splints, crutches

### Craftsdwarf's Workshop

Moodable skills: **Bone Carver · Engraver · Stone Crafter · Wood Crafter**

Also the **default fallback** for any dwarf with no moodable skill at all — they end up here with the basic craft item set.

- **Bone Carver (bone):** leggings, greaves, gauntlets, helms, shields, instruments, toys, doors, beds, chairs, tables, statues, chests, bins, armor stands, weapon racks, cabinets, coffins, floodgates, hatches, grates, chains, cages, animal traps, figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, weapons, trap components
- **Bone Carver (shell):** leggings, gauntlets, helms, figurines, amulets, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, chains, cages, animal traps, instruments, toys
- **Engraver:** figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, goblets, instruments, toys *(engravers craft a portable item here — they don't engrave a wall as the artifact)*
- **Stone Crafter:** figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, goblets, instruments, toys
- **Wood Crafter:** figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, goblets, instruments, toys

### Jeweler's Workshop

Moodable skills: **Gem Cutter · Gem Setter**

About **50% of the time** the mood produces a "perfect gem" (cut-gem artifact). Otherwise: doors, beds, chairs, tables, statues, boxes, armor stands, weapon racks, cabinets, coffins, floodgates, hatches, grates, figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, chains, flasks, goblets, cages, barrels, buckets, animal traps, windows, instruments, toys.

### Glass Furnace (or Magma Glass Furnace)

Moodable skills: **Glassmaker**

- doors, beds, chairs, tables, statues, boxes, armor stands, weapon racks, cabinets, coffins, floodgates, hatches, grates, figurines, amulets, scepters, crowns, rings, earrings, bracelets, chains, flasks, goblets, cages, barrels, buckets, animal traps, windows, instruments, toys

### Leather Works

Moodable skills: **Leatherworker · Tanner**

- dresses, shirts, tunics, togas, vests, robes, coats, cloaks, capes, trousers, loincloths, thongs, skirts, braies, leggings, gloves, mittens, socks, sandals, shoes, chausses, boots, caps, hoods, masks, turbans, head veils, face veils, headscarves, helms, shields, bags, backpacks, quivers, instruments

### Clothier's Shop

Moodable skills: **Clothier · Weaver**

*Weaver moods land at the Clothier's, not the Loom.*

- dresses, shirts, tunics, togas, vests, robes, coats, cloaks, capes, trousers, loincloths, thongs, skirts, braies, gloves, mittens, socks, sandals, shoes, chausses, caps, hoods, masks, turbans, head veils, face veils, headscarves, bags, ropes

### Bowyer's Workshop

Moodable skills: **Bowyer**

- crossbows, bows, blowguns

### Mechanic's Workshop

Moodable skills: **Mechanic**

- mechanisms

### Fell-mood only: Butcher's Shop / Tanner

Moodable skills: **Bone Carver · Leatherworker · Tanner** — *only when the mood goes fell*. The dwarf murders someone and uses the corpse to craft a bone or leather item appropriate to one of the above skill paths.

### What this is useful for

- **Skills that can't mood at all:** Cook, Brewer, Farmer, Hunter, Fisher, Trapper, Dyer, Soap Maker, Architect, Pump Operator, military, social, and medical skills. Dwarves with only these skills fall back to the Craftsdwarf's Workshop default item set — I don't pre-stock mood materials for these labors.
- **Workshops that never host moods:** Loom, Smelter, Kiln, Wood Furnace, Ashery, Still, Kitchen, Fishery, Farmer's Workshop, Quern/Millstone, Dyer, Soap Maker, Press, Paper Maker. Nothing to plan for here.
- **Restricted-output workshops worth flagging:** **Bowyer** only produces bows / crossbows / blowguns, and **Mechanic** only produces mechanisms. If I don't want my legendary artifact to be a wooden crossbow or a stone mechanism, I keep those two skills off my high-skill rotation so they aren't the dwarf's *highest* moodable skill when a mood lands.

Reference: [DF Wiki — Strange mood](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Strange_mood).
