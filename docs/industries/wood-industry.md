## Wood-only items

The items that *only* wood can make — everything else in the game has a non-wood alternative somewhere:

- **Beds** (artifact beds from strange moods are the one exception)
- **Water wheels**
- **Windmills**
- **Axles** (mechanical power transfer)
- **Siege engine parts** (catapult and ballista components)

What I plan around: **beds scale with population** — every dwarf wants their own bedroom eventually, so wood demand grows with each migrant wave. The other four come up the moment I commit to a pump stack, a mill, or a siege engine, and they're one-time spends.

### Things I assumed needed wood but don't

These come up as wood-only in my head but actually have substitutes:

- **Barrels, bins, buckets, large pots, wheelbarrows, minecarts, cages** — all forgeable from metal at the forge. Rock pots substitute for barrels in food storage; metal cages substitute for wooden ones in trap corridors.
- **Training weapons, bolts, crossbows** — also from bone; crossbows can be metal.
- **Splints, crutches** — also from bone.

So the "I need wood for this" list for embark and year-1 planning is short. Population drives bed demand; infrastructure drives the rest.

### Special-purpose woods

- **Nether-cap** (Cavern 3, dark indigo, 550) — *always room-temperature*, never burns, never melts. The only practical wood I'll put anywhere near magma — wooden minecarts, hatches, mechanisms in a magma flow path, hospital crutches for the militia after a fire.
- **Glumprong** (Cavern 3, purple, 1200) — heaviest cavern wood, more than twice the density of standard. Impact-weapon momentum on training mauls and wooden hammers is noticeably better than other woods — still no edge (wood is wood), but blunts hit harder.
- **Blood thorn** (evil biome, crimson, 1250) — the densest wood, period. Same impact-weapon case as glumprong, with crimson furniture as a styling bonus. Only grows in evil biomes — a real reason to take an embark adjacent to one.
- **Feather tree** (surface, cream, 100) — *lightest* wood, 1/5 the density of standard. High-volume crafts and totems for the trade depot get hauled faster per item, which matters when caravans are imminent. Training crossbows from feather wood also keep the recruit militia from carrying a brick.
- **Tower-cap** (Cavern 1, white, 600) — the workhorse cavern wood. Grows fast under glass; this is what my underground tree farm is made of. White-on-white furniture also pairs nicely with marble or chalk floors.
- **Tunnel tube** (Cavern 1, violet, 500) — the other common Cavern 1 species. Same farming profile as tower-cap, different color if I want variety.
- **Highwood** — listed in the raws but extremely rare in practice; mostly mod or worldgen-edge cases. Not something I plan around.
