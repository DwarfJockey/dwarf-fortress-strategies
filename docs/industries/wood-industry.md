## Wood-only items

The items that *only* wood can make — everything else in the game has a non-wood alternative somewhere:

- **Beds** (artifact beds from strange moods are the one exception)
- **Water wheels**
- **Windmills**
- **Axles** (mechanical power transfer)
- **Siege engine parts** (catapult and ballista components)

What I plan around: **beds scale with population** — every dwarf wants their own bedroom eventually, so wood demand grows with each migrant wave. The other four come up the moment I commit to a pump stack, a mill, or a siege engine, and they're one-time spends.

### Things I assumed needed wood but don't

These come up as wood-only in my head but actually have substitutes:

- **Barrels, bins, buckets, large pots, wheelbarrows, minecarts, cages** — all forgeable from metal at the forge. Rock pots substitute for barrels in food storage; metal cages substitute for wooden ones in trap corridors.
- **Training weapons, bolts, crossbows** — also from bone; crossbows can be metal.
- **Splints, crutches** — also from bone.

So the "I need wood for this" list for embark and year-1 planning is short. Population drives bed demand; infrastructure drives the rest.
