## Sealing vs. opening

=== "Sealed"

    Wall off the cavern entirely. No tower-cap, no cave wheat, no GCS silk — but no forgotten beasts either.

=== "Opened"

    Drop a fortified airlock. Trade access to underground plants, pets, and silk for the constant risk of FBs and trolls.
